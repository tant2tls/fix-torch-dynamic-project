Stock behaviour of the *decomposition*; unaffected by the three patches.

```

nearest 448->192: 7/192 rows differ; first [(27, 62, 63)]

nearest 37->74: 0/74 rows differ; first []          <-- A100 reported 36/74
nearest-exact 384->363: 2/363 rows differ; first [(60, 63, 64)]
```

⚠️ **NEW FINDING, 2026-08-28 — the `37->74` case is H100-clean but A100-wrong.**
Three of the four ratios reproduce identically on both architectures; `37->74`
differs on 36/74 rows on A100-SXM4-80GB and on 0/74 here. That is consistent with
the root cause being an *approximate hardware divide* whose ULP behaviour is
SM-version dependent, and it is a useful detail for the issue — but it means the
repro must **not** be filed with `37->74` as the headline case. Lead with
`448->192` and `384->363`, which reproduce on both.

Ratios to quote as architecture-independent (A100 + H100): `448->192` (7 rows),
`384->363` (2 rows), `nearest-exact 384->363` (2 rows).
